R.I.P. CapCut? YouTube’s AI Video Editor Might Just Change the Game

YouTube Create is a free AI-powered video editing app built to take you from ideation to upload without leaving the platform.

Let’s be honest — video editing can be a headache. Jumping between apps, figuring out timelines, fixing captions, exporting, re-uploading — all of this adds up. But YouTube stepped in with something that could seriously shake things up.

YouTube Create — YouTube’s new AI-powered video editing app that’s built to take you from moments of ideation to uploading without ever leaving the platform. And yes, this move puts tools like CapCut on notice.

So… What Exactly Is YouTube Create?

The goal is simple: create, edit, and publish in one place — no extra apps, no unnecessary friction.

YouTube Create is a free mobile app (now available on both Android and iOS) made specifically for creators. Think of it as YouTube saying, “Why should you leave our ecosystem to make content for our platform?”

Inside this app, you get auto-captions, royalty-free music, filters, transitions, and effects, plus tools optimized for Shorts and long-form videos. But the real deal? AI integrated into everything.

It’s powered by Google’s massive AI/ML infrastructure, including tech coming out of DeepMind — specifically models like Veo 3. From early previews, the interface is clean and beginner-friendly: drag-and-drop timelines, real-time previews, and smart suggestions that adapt to how you edit.

Here’s Where Things Get Interesting

YouTube Create uses Veo 3 Fast, a generative AI model from Google DeepMind that can turn simple prompts into high-quality video content.

1. Edit With AI — Upload your raw clips, and the AI can pick key moments, suggest cuts, add transitions, and sync music automatically. For new creators, this can cut editing time by up to 80%.

2. AI-Generated Clips & Backgrounds — Want a “futuristic city in the rain”? Type it in. Veo generates it — motion, atmosphere, and all. You can even restyle existing footage.

3. Speech-to-Song & AI Dubbing — You can remix speech into music or auto-dub your videos into multiple languages with better lip-sync than we’ve seen before. That’s huge for global creators.

Why Creators Should Care

From a user-experience standpoint, YouTube Create removes a ton of friction. With apps like CapCut, you still have to export your video, upload it separately, and manage everything outside YouTube. Create cuts all of that out — edit and publish instantly, with analytics available right in the tool.

For creators, this means more efficiency, more accessibility (auto-captions and multilingual tools), and better collaboration (up to five collaborators per video).

So… Is This the End of CapCut?

Not exactly — but it’s definitely pressure. CapCut is still fantastic, especially for TikTok-style edits, templates, and cross-platform content. But YouTube Create has one killer advantage: native integration.

If YouTube is your main platform, being able to edit, publish, and track performance in one app is a serious win. That alone could save creators hours of extra work every week.

In reality, nobody truly “loses” here. Competition forces innovation — and creators benefit the most. The future of content creation is faster, smarter, and way more creator-friendly.

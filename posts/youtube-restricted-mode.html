How to Turn Off Restricted Mode on YouTube

A clear step-by-step guide for mobile and desktop.

Restricted Mode on YouTube limits the content you can see. Here’s how to turn it off on both mobile and desktop.

For Mobile Device Users

  1. Open the YouTube app on your device.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap General.
  5. Scroll down to Restricted Mode and make sure it is disabled (the toggle should be gray, not light blue).

For Desktop Users

  1. Go to www.youtube.com in your browser.
  2. Click your profile picture in the top right corner.
  3. Click Restricted Mode.
  4. Toggle it off (the control turns gray when disabled).

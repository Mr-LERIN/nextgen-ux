What Happened at Apple WWDC 2023 Event?

A detailed look at the keynote and other announcements from June 5–9, 2023.

Apple’s Worldwide Developers Conference (WWDC) is an annual event where the company showcases its latest software and hardware innovations for developers. The 2023 conference was held from June 5–9, and it was a packed week full of announcements.

Keynote

The keynote address on Monday, June 5 kicked off the conference. Apple CEO Tim Cook took the stage to introduce a number of new products and features, including:

  • Apple Vision Pro — A new spatial computing platform that powers AR and VR experiences on Apple devices.
  • New Macs — Two new MacBook Air models with M2 chips, a new Mac Studio with M2 Max chip, and a new Studio Display monitor.
  • iOS 17 — The latest version of Apple’s mobile operating system, with new features like Live Text, Visual Look Up, and Stage Manager.
  • iPadOS 16 — New features including Stage Manager, Collaboration, and Reference Mode.
  • watchOS 9 — Atrial fibrillation detection, medication tracking, and sleep stages.
  • macOS Ventura — Stage Manager, Continuity Camera, and Live Text on the desktop.

Other Announcements

Throughout the week Apple also announced:

  • Swift Playgrounds 4 — Making it easier for kids to learn to code on iPad.
  • RealityKit 3 — A new version of Apple’s AR development platform.
  • Metal 3 — Making it easier to create high-performance graphics experiences.

Overall, Apple WWDC 2023 showcased the company’s latest software and hardware innovations. The announcements made during the conference will have a major impact on the way we use our devices — and they continue to shape the platforms developers build on today.

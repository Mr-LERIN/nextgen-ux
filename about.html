About NextGEN UX

NextGEN UX is a focused technology and design publication — a subsidiary of NextGEN TECH. We exist to make complex technical topics clear, practical, and useful.

Our work lives at the intersection of DIY technology, how-to guides, and next-generation user experience. Whether you’re troubleshooting a real-world problem, building something yourself, or thinking carefully about how people interact with technology, this is the place for clear writing and actionable insight.

We believe good design and good engineering should feel inevitable — not flashy, not over-explained, just quietly better. That philosophy shapes every guide, deep dive, and critique we publish.

Currently the writing lives on Medium. This site is the next step: a standalone home built for long-form reading, better structure, and a design language that matches the quality of the work.

More posts, deeper series, and new formats are coming. If you want to stay close to the work, the newsletter is the best place to do that.
